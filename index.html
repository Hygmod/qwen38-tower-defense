How to play

Stop the horde from crossing the maze. You start with 20 lives and 200 gold.

Goal

Enemies spawn at the green gate (left) and walk the path to the amber gate (right). Each one that gets through costs a life — lose all 20 and it's over. Routes are pathfound live around your towers, so where you build bends the maze.

Building

Pick a tower, then click an empty cell. You can't build on the path or on another tower, and any placement that seals the maze is rejected for free.

  • Gun (50g) — cheap, fast, single-target. Your workhorse.
  • Frost (75g) — weak hits, but chills enemies to half speed for 2s. Great vs. fast scouts.
  • Cannon (100g) — slow blast with area damage. Melts swarms.

Waves & economy

Press Start Wave (or Space). Count and health grow every wave; the Next row shows the exact lineup coming, so you can prepare. Kills and wave clears pay gold, and saved gold banks 5% interest (max 30g) on each clear — hoarding a little for a boss wave pays off.

Every 5th wave ends with a Boss: very tough, big gold reward. Concentrate fire or slow it.

Upgrading & targeting

Click a placed tower to select it. Upgrade raises damage and range at an escalating cost; Sell refunds 70% of everything invested. Set each tower's targeting mode:

  • First — closest to the exit (default). Last — rearmost.
  • Strong — toughest enemy: focus fire on brutes and bosses.
  • Close — nearest to the tower.

Counterplay

  • Scouts (fast, wave 3+) outrun slow towers — frost or high fire-rate.
  • Brutes (tanky, wave 5+) soak damage — Strong targeting or a kill box.
  • Kill box — seal an enemy into a pocket (entry→exit still connects). It stops and dissolves after 5s if unshot, but towers in range can shoot it for free gold.
